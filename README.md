# 💗 Hi Mumma

### A Pregnancy Care Journey Companion

> **Making every step of pregnancy easier to understand, track, and follow.**

Hi Mumma is a maternal-care journey companion designed to help expecting mothers stay organized and informed throughout pregnancy and the postpartum period.

Instead of overwhelming mothers with medical information, Hi Mumma transforms the pregnancy journey into a simple, stage-based experience with personalized journey guidance, health tracking, educational content, records management, and supportive reminders.

---

## 🌸 Why Hi Mumma?

Pregnancy involves multiple appointments, tests, supplements, vaccinations, health records, lifestyle changes, and important milestones.

Between clinical visits, mothers may have difficulty keeping track of:

- What stage of pregnancy they are currently in
- Upcoming pregnancy milestones
- Tests and ultrasound information
- Supplements and vaccinations
- Health measurements
- Medical records
- Pregnancy-related educational information
- Postpartum follow-up

**Hi Mumma brings these elements together into one simple pregnancy journey.**

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 🤰 Pregnancy Journey

A stage-based pregnancy roadmap covering:

- Start
- First Trimester
- Second Trimester
- Third Trimester
- Delivery
- Postpartum

The journey adapts its visual content based on the pregnancy week.

---

## 🗓️ Week-wise Pregnancy Experience

The application presents pregnancy information according to the mother's current pregnancy week.

The pregnancy-development visual system supports different developmental stages:

- Week 1
- Week 4
- Week 8
- Week 12
- Weeks 13–24
- Weeks 25–36
- Weeks 37–40

This allows the visual experience to evolve throughout pregnancy rather than displaying the same static content.

---

## 💗 Mother Avatar

Hi Mumma uses a premium 3D-style pregnant mother avatar as part of the main experience.

The avatar is designed to feel like an application character rather than a conventional photograph.

It includes subtle:

- Entrance animations
- Floating/breathing motion
- Parallax effects
- Interactive micro-interactions

---

## 👶 Baby Reminder Avatar

A separate baby avatar is used for reminders and emotional interactions.

The baby character can visually respond to different reminder states:

- 😊 Normal
- 🥺 Reminder approaching
- 😢 Overdue
- 💗 Completed

Example messages:

> "Mumma, don't forget! 🥺"

> "Mummaaa… you forgot! 🥺"

> "Yay, Mumma! 💗"

The reminder baby is intentionally separate from the pregnancy-development visual.

---

# 📊 Health Tracking

## 🩺 Blood Pressure Log

Users can record:

- Systolic pressure
- Diastolic pressure
- Date
- Time
- Optional notes

The application provides:

- Reading history
- Recent readings
- Weekly tracking
- Twice-weekly tracking progress

The module is designed for **record keeping and tracking**, not medical diagnosis.

---

## 🩸 Blood Sugar Tracking

Users can record:

- Blood sugar value
- Date
- Time
- Optional notes

The interface provides:

- Recent readings
- History
- Simple trend visualization
- Tracking over time

The application does not independently diagnose or recommend treatment.

---

## 💊 Supplement Tracker

Pregnancy supplements can be organized and tracked.

The application includes:

### IFA Tablet
**Iron + Folic Acid**

Supplement information is presented for educational and tracking purposes without prescribing dosage or changing medication instructions.

---

# 🧪 Tests & Ultrasound

The application organizes pregnancy-related testing information, including:

- Important tests
- Ultrasound information
- First ultrasound information
- Third ultrasound
- Fourth ultrasound
- Hb/CBC tracking
- Clinician-approved/configurable testing schedule placeholders

The purpose is to help users organize and understand their care journey.

---

# 💉 Vaccination Tracker

A dedicated vaccination-tracking experience helps users organize pregnancy-related vaccination information.

---

# 👣 Fetal Movement Tracking

Users can record fetal movement observations as part of their pregnancy tracking.

This feature is intentionally designed as a tracking tool.

It does not:

- Diagnose medical conditions
- Calculate medical risk
- Classify the pregnancy as safe/unsafe
- Provide emergency decisions

---

# 📚 Education Hub

The Education section provides stage-specific educational information such as:

- What to expect during pregnancy
- Maternal changes
- Pregnancy-stage tips
- Travel safety
- Diet and lifestyle information
- Trimester explanation
- GI symptoms such as nausea and constipation
- Medicine side-effect education
- Government schemes
- Delivery bag information
- Postpartum information
